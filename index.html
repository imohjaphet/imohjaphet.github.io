Before Automation

Referrals tracked across scattered WhatsApp chats and email threads. Admins calculated each ambassador's 20% commission by hand every month — slowly, and with frequent disputes. Nobody was notified when the ₦20,000 acceptance fee actually landed.

After Automation

Every referral is logged to one Airtable CRM the moment the form is submitted. Commission is calculated by an Airtable formula, never by hand. Marking a fee "Paid" notifies the ambassador within two minutes, and monthly reports send themselves.

Key Engineering Decision: The 20% commission formula lives directly inside Airtable, not inside Zapier's transient logic. This means one formula edit updates every record at once, maintaining a single source of truth and supporting tiered rates without altering any Zap.
