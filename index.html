Featured Capstone Build · Zapier + Airtable

Ambassador Commission Automation

A capstone build for Cybervast AI Automation's referral program: three connected Zaps that take a referral from a single Google Form submission all the way to a confirmed, correctly-calculated commission — with zero manual steps in between.

ZAP 1 · Instant Webhook

Lead Ingestion & Binding

Captures Google Forms submissions, validates referral credentials, creates a normalized record in Airtable CRM, and dispatches immediate tracking emails to the referral and ambassador.

ZAP 2 · 2-Min View Poll

₦20,000 Fee Verification

Monitors Airtable for fee status marked "Paid". Triggers Airtable formula calculating 20% commission (₦4,000), locks row against duplicate runs, and confirms payout to the ambassador in <120 seconds.

ZAP 3 · Monthly Cron

Reconciliation & Statements

Triggers on the 1st of every month at 08:00 AM. Aggregates prior month commissions per ambassador, compiles itemized breakdown statements, and emails them automatically via Gmail.

Before Automation

Referrals tracked across scattered WhatsApp chats and email threads. Admins calculated each ambassador's 20% commission by hand every month — slowly, and with frequent disputes. Nobody was notified when the ₦20,000 acceptance fee actually landed.

After Automation

Every referral is logged to one Airtable CRM the moment the form is submitted. Commission is calculated by an Airtable formula, never by hand. Marking a fee "Paid" notifies the ambassador within two minutes, and monthly reports send themselves.

Key Engineering Decision: The 20% commission formula lives directly inside Airtable, not inside Zapier's transient logic. This means one formula edit updates every record at once, maintaining a single source of truth and supporting tiered rates without altering any Zap.
